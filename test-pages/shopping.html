Best MacBook Monitors 2026

Top Picks

After testing dozens of monitors with M-series MacBooks, we've identified the best options for different use cases and budgets.

LG UltraFine 5K

The LG UltraFine 5K remains the gold standard for Mac users. Its pixel-perfect resolution matches the MacBook Pro display, and Thunderbolt connectivity provides single-cable setup.

Dell U2723QE

For budget-conscious buyers, the Dell U2723QE offers excellent color accuracy and USB-C connectivity at a lower price point. The IPS Black panel delivers deeper blacks than standard IPS.

Apple Studio Display

Apple's own Studio Display integrates seamlessly with macOS, offering Center Stage camera, spatial audio, and a stunning 5K panel. Premium pricing but unmatched integration.
