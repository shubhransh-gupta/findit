AI Agents: The Future of Autonomous Computing

What Are AI Agents?

AI agents are autonomous systems that can perceive their environment, make decisions, and take actions to achieve specific goals. Unlike traditional software, agents can adapt and learn from their experiences.

Agent Architectures

Modern AI agents typically combine large language models with tool use, memory systems, and planning capabilities. The ReAct pattern interleaves reasoning and acting for complex task completion.

Multi-Agent Systems

Multiple AI agents can collaborate to solve problems that would be difficult for a single agent. Agent orchestration frameworks manage communication and task delegation between agents.

Applications

AI agents are being deployed in customer service, code generation, research assistance, and autonomous navigation. The key challenge is ensuring reliable and safe agent behavior.
